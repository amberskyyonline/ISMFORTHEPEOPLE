Frequently Asked Questions

Cancellations & Order Changes

If you need to cancel or change an order please email: ismforthepeople@gmail.com.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. International orders are shipped via UPS Worldwide. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination. If you do not see your country in the shipping options at checkout, please email us at ismforthepeople@gmail.com.

Are duties and taxes included?

International customers are responsible for any customs fees, duties, or local VAT levied by their home country upon import.

What is your return policy?

All sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds once an order is placed.

How long does processing take?

Orders are processed and dispatched within 3–5 business days from New York City. Tracking information is sent immediately upon fulfillment.
